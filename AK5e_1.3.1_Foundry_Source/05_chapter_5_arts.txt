# Chapter 5: Arts

In the world of Terra, Arts is essentially magic with scientific backing. Arts is the use of energy produced by Originium to alter matter and energy, manipulating and transforming them into new forms like electricity or radiation; more esoteric Arts allow the manipulation of more conceptual things, such as memory and emotion, or the healing of wounds. The innate ability of a person to wield Arts is based on their Originium Arts Assimilation, a genetic trait of each person. Most Terrans are capable of wielding rudimentary Arts, enough to activate the myriad Arts Units and systems based on Arts that pervade Terran society. 
:
Using Arts in this way is very different from being a Caster, however. Casters wield Arts at a much greater Level, strengthening their naturally high Arts Assimilation with study and practice. They are people who are both naturally gifted and have put in great effort to master their craft, and are thus highly valued all over Terra.
:
Thus, while it is not too dissimilar from magic in Dungeons and Dragons, it is different in enough ways to matter. This section will go over the ways in which it differs, and some of the why.

#### Learning and Preparing Arts

As is usual, before you can cast an Art, you must first know it. You can learn Arts through many different ways; Species Features, Class or Branch Features, Background Features, etc. A major difference between Dungeons and Dragons and AK5e is that <u>once you know an Art, it is always prepared.</u>

#### Arts Units/Casting Units
:
Casting Arts involves the caster harnessing the power of Originium to alter or affect aspects of the world. However, in order to do so, the Caster typically requires a medium; a tool containing Originium through which they can channel their thoughts to generate the intended effect, known as an Arts Unit.
:
Arts Units can vary greatly in size, shape, and function; they are as common as computers in our world, and serve many of the same purposes. Most Terrans are capable of extremely rudimentary Arts, and can operate these devices.
:
A Casting Unit is an advanced Arts Unit that Casters use to access their advanced Arts; all Casting Units are Arts Units, but not all Arts Units are Casting Units. Casting Units can also vary in size, shape, and function, in aid of amplifying, stabilising, or directing the Caster's innate ability.
:
<u>Unless stated otherwise, to cast Arts, a Creature must have a Casting Unit equipped. This is any equipment with the Casting Unit Property.</u>

{{note
##### Oripathy and Arts
As stated, Arts uses the energy of Originium to achieve its effect, and requires a Casting Unit because it contains the required Originium. Those with Oripathy have Originium as a part of their very body, and can use this Originium to cast Arts. This is not without consequences, however, as it stimulates the growth of the crystals inside them, worsening their condition and shortening their lifespan, sometimes dramatically.
:
See New Mechanics: Oripathy for more information.
}}
:
#### No Material Components
Casting Arts requires no Material Components. Any Art that would normally have this requirement no longer does.
:
{{note
##### Arts Damage
All Damage dealt by Arts is considered to be Arts Damage unless stated otherwise.
}}

## Arts Points
Creatures no longer have Spell Slots, but instead have Arts Points, the exact amount of which is determined mostly by Class, or sometimes Branch, Level (in the case of Player Characters) or GM Discretion (in the case of Non Player Characters).
:
Casting an Art requires the caster to pay a cost in Arts Points equal to the Level they wish to cast the Art at. If they do not have enough Arts Points, they cannot cast the Art at that Level.
:
The maximum Level at which a caster can use an Art is controlled by their Character Level or CR; while a Creature will have enough Arts Points to cast a 9th Level Art fairly early into their career, they cannot do so until their Maximum Art Level is 9.
{{note
##### 9th Level Arts
9th Level Arts are the peak of what a Caster can achieve, capable of world changing events. For this reason, a Creature can only cast a single 9th Level Art per Long Rest, regardless of remaining Arts Points.
}}

#### Psychic Arts
:
Perhaps the most rare of the "standard" Arts, Psychic Arts interfere with the consciousness of the target by altering nervous impulses in their brain and body. In this way, the Caster can control, to a certain extent, what the target percieves, and how they feel. Less subtly, they can also directly cause them harm through this same method; altering brain chemistry can lead to sharp and severe physiological consequences. Because of these facts, and the subtly of their Arts, Psychic Casters are the subject of fear and suspicion; a fear that only intensifies the more aware you are of them.

#### Spatial Arts
:
Spatial Arts, as the name would suggest, involve manipulating the physical properties of an object; specifically its position. Their method of doing so is not just simple telekinesis, though it is certainly involved, but also through manipulating the effect of gravity of an object. By decreasing the weight of an object, it makes it easier to throw or otherwise launch. By reversing gravitational force in an area, they can exclude objects and people both from passing through an area. As a result of these Arts, Spatial Casters are often found in supportive roles, but do not underestimate their capacity for destruction.

Some Special Arts have the Incantation Tag. The Incantation Tag is almost the opposite of the Ritual Tag; these Arts require a long time to cast by default, but this time can be decreased. #### Banshee Arts
:
Banshee Arts, like many Sarkaz Arts, are not so much Arts in the modern context as they are rituals. Rituals require a goal, and most notably, a cost. How the Caster defines these things is a crucial component in the end result. For Banshees, they manifest their Arts through word and song; in other words, language. To a Banshee, the power of language is in its rules and constraints; in fact and definition, not interpretation and imagination. Through imposing these limitations on language to themselves, they can pay the "cost" for their Rituals in a way less mortal than other Sarkaz.
:
The end result of this is that Banshee Arts simply cause things to happen; the limitations imposed ensure that the intended result either happens, or it doesn't. There is no room for doubt in the words of a Banshee.

#### Healing Arts
:
Healing Arts differ from typical Arts in that their method of application is not easily defined. The body is extremely complex, but underneath it all, it is ultimately just nutrients and minerals, both of which can be acted on by Arts. The ways in which Arts can be used to promote healing are thus great and varied; some vastly promote natural healing, some physically close wounds and knit bone back together, others alter the chemistry of the body, and so on. Many of the principles practised by practioners of other forms of Arts find a use in Healing Arts also; what they all have in common is that they all require an advanced understanding of the physiology of the target, lest you do more harm than good.

#### Witchcraft Arts
:
"Witchcraft" is, in actuality, a derogatory term for the unique Arts of the Sarkaz. The official term is "Incantation", but outside of scholars, and those that deal in such incantations, Witchcraft is a more prevalent term. 
:
What divides Incantations from typical Arts is the methodology. "Classical Arts", as they are sometimes called, uses the energy contained within Originium to manipulate energy in the world; essentially allowing the Caster to impose their will on the world around them. In contrast, Incantations do not rely on the energy of Originium, but instead through defining both the "intention" of the Incantation and "cost" that will be paid. The Caster of an Incantation defines these things through their own understanding and conventional wisdom. As a result, Incantations are highly ritualistic in nature, far more than Classical Arts, as tradition and rules help define these costs and intentions.  
:
Because these Incantations do not require Originium in the same way Classical Arts do, almost every Sarkaz has some limited affinity with them. Most Sarkaz, however, will steer away from such rituals without extreme cause. Just as the different Sarkaz clans have penned meaning and cost to their own Incantations, millennia and suffering has assigned its own cost to these unshackled Incantations, and it is a heavy price to pay.
:
