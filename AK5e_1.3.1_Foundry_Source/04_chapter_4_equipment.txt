# Chapter 4: Equipment

Right place, right time;

That is the modus operandi for equipment. If you do not have the right equipment when and where you need it, you may as well have never had it. But just as this equipment can be defined by its wielder, so too does it define them.

## Currency on Terra
:
While a majority of Terran nations have their own currencies, there is one that stands out: Lungmen Dollars, or LMD as it is commonly known. Terra's political and financial landscapes are volatile and unstable; since the War of the Four Emperors and its consequences, many nations sought common ground on which to conduct fair trade and fund rebuilding efforts. The currency of neutral Lungmen, already an international centre of trade, came to be that common ground. It is a relatively stable currency, and is the main currency used in international transactions. As such, it is accepted in all but the most remote locations.

### New Properties

AK5e introduces a number of new Special Properties for weapons. 

#### Bound
Weapons with this Property have been bound by a Flinger.
:
#### Brace
To make an Attack using a weapon you are wielding with the Brace Property, you must also pay 10ft of Movement. 
:
If the weapon also has the Loading Property, this cost is increased to 30ft. 
:
#### Casting Unit
Weapons with this Special Property can be used to cast Arts; if you are wielding a Weapon with this Property, you may ignore the Somatic Components of Arts.
:
#### Conductive (X)
While you have a weapon with the Conductive (X) Property Equipped, you gain +X to your Spell Attack Rolls and Spell Save DC. If you have multiple weapons with this property Equipped, only the largest value of X is applied. 
:
#### Couch
If you make an Attack with a weapon with the Couch Property while it is not your Turn, that Attack deals additional Damage equal to your Proficiency Bonus. 
:
#### Crush (X)
If you hit a Creature with an Attack with this Property, the Damage of that Attack cannot be reduced to less than X by Damage Reduction or Resistance (Immunities apply as normal). If the rolled Damage of the Attack is less than X, it instead cannot be reduced any further. 
:
#### CQC
You do not suffer Disadvantage on Ranged Attack Rolls using a Weapon with this Property against a Creature within 5 feet of you.
:
#### Deadly
The first time each Turn you hit with an Attack using a weapon with the Deadly Property, you may cause that Attack to deal additional Damage equal to your Proficiency Bonus.
:
#### Firearm
This weapon is a Terran firearm, which operate on Originium Arts. You do not need to be able to cast any Arts to use weapons with this Property, but any effect that prevents the casting of Arts also prevents use of this weapon.


\column
#### Rapid Fire
If you have attacked with a weapon with this Property this Turn, you may make an additional Attack with that weapon as a Bonus Action. If you are wielding two weapons with this Property, you may instead make an Attack with each of those Weapons as a Bonus Action. However, you do not add your Ability Modifier to the Damage Roll for Attacks you make using this Property.

#### Returning
If you make a Ranged Attack using the Thrown Property of this weapon, it returns to your hand after the Attack. If the Attack Roll result is a Natural 1, the weapon does not return, and falls at the feet of the target of that Attack.

#### Splash (X)
You do not make Attack Rolls when attacking using a weapon with this Property. Instead, choose a point within the weapon's range; all Creatures within X feet of that Point must succeed on a Dexterity Saving Throw against your Martial DC, suffering the weapon’s normal Damage plus your Ability Score Modifier on a failed Save, or half that amount on a successful Save. If you are not Proficient with the weapon, Creatures have Advantage on this Saving Throw.
:
#### Spread
When you hit with an Attack using a weapon with this Property against a Creature within its normal range, you may deal Piercing Damage equal to the Ability Modifier used for the Attack to a different Creature within 5 feet of the original Creature.

## Armour
:

**Category:** All Armour is either Light, Medium, or Heavy Armour. The distinction matters primarily for how long it takes to Don and Doff the Armour, but some Features may also reference this. 

**Armour Class (AC):** Your Base AC is equal to this value while you are wearing this Armour.

**Damage Reduction (DR):** When you take Damage from an Attack or Art while you are wearing this Armour, it is reduced by this value, to a minimum of 1. This DR only applies to the type of Damage specified.

**Strength:** If your Strength is less than this value, your Speed is reduced by 10ft while you are wearing this Armour.

**Special:** What other effects are conferred when you are wearing this Armour.

## Toolkits and other Equipment
:
AK5e has a number of Toolkits available, representing a variety of skills and professions that characters may be able to practice in the field. The description of a Toolkit includes the following information:
:
**Starting Charges:** How many Charges this Toolkit has when it is unused. Certain uses of a Toolkit will consume these Charges; Uses that require Charges cannot be performed if the Toolkit does not have enough Charges.
:
**Uses:** What you can do with this Toolkit, as well as the cost in Charges, if there is one. You do not need to proficient in the Toolkit to use these options.
{{note
##### Alternative Toolkit Uses
The uses for each Toolkit listed here are not to be considered an exhaustive list. If you have an idea for a way to use a kit that is not one of the proscribed ways, raise it with the GM; ultimately, they have final jurisdiction on what you are and are not allowed to do outside of what is listed.
}}
:
**Proficient Uses:** What you can do with this Toolkit, as well as the cost in Charges, if there is one. You must be proficient in the Toolkit to use these options.
:
**Variants:** This entry appears if the tool has variants, which are listed. Each requires a separate proficiency.
:

{{note
##### Refill Cost
You don't need to buy a whole new Kit to regain Charges for a Kit you already have. Instead, anywhere you could buy that Kit, you can restore 1 Charge back to a Toolkit by paying its Refill Cost.

#### Art Kit
:
Just as every artist is different, every Art Kit is different, suited to the person who put it together. A street artist's kit may be full of spray cans, while a "traditional" artist's may be full of brush and canvas. Ultimately however, artists have more in common than they do apart; a desire to send a message, and the vision to see it done.
:
**Starting Charges:** 10
:
**Uses:** 

***Create Art (2 Charges):*** As a Long Rest Activity, you can create a piece of Art; this Art can depict whatever you wish. To do so, you must make a DC13 Wisdom Check; if you are Proficient with this Kit, you may add your Proficiency Bonus to this roll. On a success, the Art you create is valuable, and can be sold for normal price (100 LMD), On a failure, you still create the Art, but its value is reduced to 0 LMD. 

***Mark (1 Charges):*** Over the course of a minute, you can quickly sketch/spray/paint a symbol or sign onto a material or the environment. 
:
**Proficient Uses:** 

***Artist's Eye:*** Your familiarity with art and its tools gives you insight into deciphering the meanings behind art. You have a +5 modifier to any Check to determine meaning or messages in art, including hidden messages and codes, as well as the mindset of the person who made that art.

***Disguise Appearance (2 Charges):*** Your expertise with your tools allows you (either doing it yourself or instructing others), as a Long Rest Activity, to paint an object, vehicle, or piece of armour into a design of your choice. This design could be camouflage, providing a bonus to Stealth Checks involving the subject, or even a disguise to disguise the subject as a member of a faction.

#### Brewing Kit
:
Alcohol is a nearly universal truth in all civilisations. It bridges gaps and opens doors in a way that words alone often cannot. The talents and tools used in creating it, however, have more than one application, turning poisoned and polluted water into lifesaving drinking water. The brewer can always find a place, or people, who need them. The Brewer's Kit contains brewing and distillation equipment, as well as instruments for monitoring the contents of fluids.
:
**Starting Charges:** 10
:
**Uses:** 

***Brew Alcohol (2 Charges):*** As a Long Rest Activity, you can begin to brew alcohol. It finishes brewing when you next complete a Long Rest; the number of servings brewed is equal to your Proficiency Bonus. To do so, when it finishes brewing, you must make a DC13 Wisdom Check; if you are Proficient in this Kit, you may add your Proficiency Bonus to this roll. On a success, the Alcohol you create is valuable, and can be sold for normal price (25 LMD), On a failure, you still create the alcohol, but it is of lower quality, and its value is reduced to 0. 

***Purify Water (1 Charge):*** Over the duration of a Short Rest, you can use the distilling tools present in this kit to purify enough water for a single person to drink for one day. 
:
**Proficient Uses:**

***Analyse Fluids:*** Modern brewing is a careful, scientific process that requires close monitoring and fine instrumentation in order to avoid contamination. Using the instruments and your knowledge, you can grant yourself a +5 bonus to any check to determine the contents of a body of liquid, including if it is poisoned/alcoholic/etc.

***Mass Purify Water (3 Charges):*** Your familiarity with the kit allows you to process vast amounts of fluid when you have concerted time to work at it. As a Long Rest Activity, you may purify enough drinking water for a number of people equal to your Wisdom Modifier + your Proficiency Bonus.
:
{{note
##### Alcohol
Alcohol has been a feature of almost every civilisation since mankind learned the art of fermentation. It can break the ice between strangers, either as a gift, or through removing their inhibitions.
:
A Creature that consumes Alcohol has a -1 Modifier to Dexterity Checks and Saving Throws, but a +1 to Charisma Checks and Wisdom Saving Throws. 
}}

#### Cooking Kit
:
Cooking food could be argued to be one of the things that defines civilisation; one of the things that divides man from animal. People with a good meal in their bellies can bear hardship that the hungry cannot, and the person that can provide this will be forever beloved. The Cooking Kit contains the tools necessary to cook away from a kitchen, in addition to a spread of long-life ingredients.
:
**Starting Charges:** 5
:
**Uses:** 

***Create Rations (1 Charge):*** Without the aid of a kitchen, sometimes just keeping people from starving to death is enough. As a Long Rest Activity, you can cook enough Rations to feed a number of people equal to your Wisdom Modifier + your Proficiency Bonus.

***Prepare Ingredients:*** As a Short Rest Activity, you can either scavenge vegetation or prepare a dead animal for consumption (if there is one available). To do so, you must make a DC13 Wisdom Check; if you are Proficient in this Kit, you may add your Proficiency Bonus to this Roll. Furthermore, if you are preparing an animal, you have Advantage on this Roll. On a success, you restore one Charge of this Kit, up to its maximum of 5. On a failure, you waste the ingredients, and do not restore a Charge.
:
**Proficient Uses:** 

***Camp Cooking (2 Charge):*** Being in the wilderness is no excuse for anyone to eat badly. As a Long Rest Activity, you can create a Meal for a number of Creatures equal to your Proficiency Bonus + Wisdom Modifier (unless it is negative). Creatures that consume the meal roll one of their Hit Dice (this does not expend their Hit Dice) and gain that many Temporary Hit Points.

***Poison Tasting:*** Your expertise with the tools of cooking gives you special insight. Using these tools, you can identify whether food is safe or dangerous to eat by succeeding on a DC13 Perception Check.

#### Engineering Kit
:
Wrenches, screwdrivers, wire cutters and strippers, blowtorches; these are the tools by which mechanics and engineers weave their "magic", turning inert metal into vehicles. The kit of an engineer is equal parts toolbox and portable scrapheap.
:
**Starting Charges:** 5
:
**Uses:** 

***Emergency Repairs (1 Charge):*** With the right tools, you can often keep a machine ticking longer than it should. Choose a Vehicle or Construct within 5 feet. If you work on it uninterrupted for 1 minute, roll a number of d4's equal to your Intelligence Modifier (or your Intelligence Modifier + your Proficiency Bonus if you are Proficient with this Kit); it regains that many Hit Points, and gains any excess as Temporary Hit Points. 

***Examine Machinery:*** The tools in this kit are useful in essentially all interactions with machinery, but especially useful when trying to figure out what has gone wrong. If you take 10 minutes to examine them, you gain a +5 Bonus to Technology Tests to determine the state and function of those constructs or machines that is not cumulative. 
:
**Proficient Uses:** 

***Automaton Automation (2 Charges):*** As a Long Rest Activity, you can prepare an Automaton (Turret) for use (Page 467). The Turret begins in its portable form. A minute after being unfolded from its portable form for the first time, a Turret created in this way dies.  You can only have one such Turret at a time, but it does not count against the maximum amount of Automatons or Drones you can control.

***Jury Rig (1 Charge):***  As a Short Rest Activity, you can "improve" a Vehicle or Construct. You can either:
- Give the target a +2 Bonus to all Driving Checks performed with it. A Vehicle or Construct can only be the target of this Proficient Use once.
- Give one Weapon on the target a +2 Bonus to Attack Rolls with that Weapon. Each Weapon on that Vehicle or Construct can only be the target of this Proficient Use once.

#### Maintenance Kit
:
As any soldier or engineer will tell you, if you fail to take care of your tools, they will fail to take care of you. The maintenance kit contains all of the necessary equipment to perform rudimentary metalwork away from a dedicated workshop; you won't make anything new, but you can keep what you have in good condition.
:
**Starting Charges:** 10
:
**Uses:** 

***Maintain Weapon (2 Charge):*** As a Short Rest Activity, you can choose a weapon to work on and maintain it back to its peak performance. That weapon gains one of the following benefits (your choice): 
- +1 to Attack Rolls using that weapon
- +1 to Damage Rolls with that weapon
- The value of the Range Property is increased by 50%.

A Weapon can only have one of these benefits at a time, and loses these benefits at the end of any fight in which they were used to make an Attack.

***Repair Weapon/Armour (2 Charges):*** As a Long Rest Activity, you can repair a damaged Weapon, Medium Armour, or Heavy Armour. To do so, you must succeed on a DC13 Strength Check; if you are Proficient in this Kit, you may add your Proficiency Bonus to this Roll. Any item that has been repaired has its value reduced to half (rounding up) of its original value.
:
On a failure, nothing happens, but the charge and time are consumed.
:
**Proficient Uses:** 

***Material Analysis:*** Your knowledge of weapons and armour gives you special insight into what events lead to what effects on the metal. If you take a minute to examine a weapon or piece of medium/heavy armour, you gain a +5 Bonus to Checks to learn about its recent history and provenance, such as how often it has been used, what caused particular marks or damage to it, or where it was likely made.

***Mobile Workshop:*** When you use the ***Repair Weapon/Armour*** or ***Maintain Weapon*** options of this Toolkit, you can instead target a number of things equal to your Strength Modifier (unless it is negative) + your Proficiency Bonus, as opposed to just one.

#### Makeover Kit

## Magic Items
Magic items within the world of Arknights are mechanically identical to those of Dungeons and Dragons, but the reason behind them is different. In Dungeons and Dragons, a Magic Item is normally something crafted in a particular arcane way, or instilled with a particularly powerful enchantment. In AK5e, while some Magic Items may indeed fall into those categories, most are instead the result of strange innovations, or are prototype technologies that are not replicable yet.
:
The following is a selection of Magic Items that may be found on Terra; it is not an exhaustive list. If you would like to try and find a Magic Item that is not listed here, consult with your DM to see if it makes sense within the Arknights setting, and where you might get it if it does.

#### Rusted Hammer
*Weapon (Hammer) or Weapon (Heavy Hammer), Rare (Attunement Required)*
:
These hammers are the symbol of the eponymous Rusthammers, an anarcho-primitivist militant group active in the Terran Barrenlands. In the sixty years since they were first reported, they have risen from minor Columbian bandits to an internationally active rebel group, often labelled terrorists by the powers that be. Anyone who wishes to join the Rusthammers is welcomed by them, on one condition; they must pass a trial proving themselves capable of surviving in the wilderness, as only those who can separate from modern living can become Rusthammers. That is what these hammers represent; the Rusthammers need not be the sharpest, only the most durable.

**Durable.** This weapon cannot be damaged unless it would be destroyed completely.

**Enduring.** After you take Damage, you can expend your Reaction to expend and roll one of your Hit Dice. You regain Hit Points equal to the amount rolled + your Constitution Modifier.

**Survivor.** When you make a Survival Check while you are attuned to this weapon and the result on the d20 is less than 8, it becomes 8. 

**Symbol.** What this weapon represents is known to many people. Openly possessing it may earn your friendship from some, and hostility from others.

#### The Black Lock and The White Key
*Weapons (Arts Staves), Artifact (Attunement required)*
:
The Black Lock and The White Key are a pair of Arts Staves crafted from the bones of a Feranmut; a type of prehistoric titan that often possess incomprehensible abilities. This particular Feranmut possessed the ability to manipulate local space and time, a trait that, while distilled, has been passed down to these staves. They are currently wielded by Mostima, a powerful fallen Sankta and Legatus of Laterano.

**Magic Staves.** These Staves have the Conductive (4) Property.

**Time Warp.** While you are attuned to both the Black Lock and White Key, the rate at which you age is halved.

**Lock and Key.** These Arts Staves do not have the Two-Handed Property, but you cannot use the following options unless you are wielding and attuned to both staves. The Lock and Key have a total of three charges, and regain 1d3 charges when you complete a Long Rest while attuned to them.
:
**Key of Chronology (1 Charge).** As an Action, all Creatures within a 30-foot cone originating from you must make a Dexterity Saving Throw againt your Spell Save DC as a pulse of temporal distortion washes over them. On a failed Save, they take 6d8 Force Damage and their Speed is halved until the end of their next Turn. On a successful Save, they take half as much Damage, and are not Slowed.

**Lock of Shattered Time (1 Charge).** As an Action, choose a Creature you can see within 100 feet; that Creature must succeed on a Charisma Saving Throw against your Spell Save DC or be locked in time. While a Creature is locked in time, it is Stunned, but cannot take Damage, gain Temporary Hit Points, or regain Hit Points. It may reattempt the Saving Throw at the end of each of its Turns, ending the effect on a success.

**Subjective Time Acceleration (1 Charge).** All allied Creatures within 30 feet of you gain 1d4 Skill Points, and their Speed is increased by 10ft until the end of their next Turn.
